ACCOUNT

Delete your Series Organizer+ account

Series Organizer+ is developed by Last Day Software. This page is the public web pathway for requesting deletion of a Series Organizer+ cloud account.

What deletion removes

  • Your Series Organizer+ Supabase authentication account
  • Your associated Series Organizer+ cloud backup snapshots

Cloud backup snapshots are cascade-deleted when the associated authentication account is deleted.

What deletion does not remove

Deleting your Series Organizer+ account does not delete your Google Account.

Device-local guest, Library and tracking data may remain on devices until you clear the app's local storage, uninstall the app, or use an explicit local-delete action when one is available.

Request deletion without the app

Public support address still required before publication.

A visible email link or secure support form must be added here before this page is used for Google Play publication.

Account verification

The deletion workflow may require enough information to safely identify the Series Organizer+ account to be removed.

Never send passwords or authentication tokens. Last Day Software will never require your Google password, Supabase access token, refresh token or other private authentication credential in a deletion request.

Processing time

The expected handling time will be published here after Last Day Software finalizes the deletion-support workflow.

Retention exceptions

If specific information must be retained because of an applicable legal obligation, that information may be retained only as required by law. No additional retention period is stated here.

Local-first use after deletion

Series Organizer+ does not require an account for normal local Library use. Deleting the cloud account therefore does not prevent local use of the application.

  • Expected deletion handling time
  • Any legally required retention exceptions
