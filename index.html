FROM SERIES ORGANIZER

The idea never went away.

Series Organizer started with a simple idea: make it easier to remember what you're watching and where you left off.

Screen Organizer takes that idea much further. It's being rebuilt from the ground up with a modern Android application, richer discovery tools and a broader vision for movies and television.

Same obsession with keeping things organized.
A completely new chapter.

YOUR LIBRARY

Always know where you left off.

Keep movies and TV shows together in one library and track exactly what you've watched.

  • Episode and movie tracking
  • Favorites and archived titles
  • Progress-aware Up Next

DISCOVER

Find something worth watching.

Search directly, browse what's popular or airing, or explore personalized recommendations based on your own library and viewing history.

  • Search and Quick Browse
  • Advanced filters

  • Personalized For You recommendations

GO DEEPER

More than a list of titles.

Screen Organizer connects your tracking with richer information about the things you're watching.

Rich details

Episodes, progress, trailers, streaming providers, artwork and more — without losing sight of where you are in the show.

Cast & credits

Explore cast members, biographies, Known For titles and full movie and television credits.

YOUR DATA

Local first. Cloud when you want it.

Screen Organizer keeps your library available locally, even when you're signed out or offline.

Optional Google sign-in lets you manually create cloud backups of your library and tracking data and restore them when you need to.

No account required to use your local library. Cloud backup is optional and does not silently replace or upload your data.
